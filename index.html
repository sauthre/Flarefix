Kyun Flarefix?

Aaj India mein TV media ek tamasha ban chuka hai. Views aur TRP ki daud mein studio chillane ki jagah ban gaye hain — uchhal-kood, shor-sharaaba, aur khule taur par pakshpaat. Khabar kam bachi hai, hungama zyada.

Hum maante hain ki khabar ka kaam faisala sunana nahi hai. Khabar ka kaam hai — tathya janta ke saamne rakhna. Janta, aam log, khud samajhdar hain: woh tathyon ko dekhenge, samjhenge, aur apna faisala khud lenge. Unhe ungli pakad kar chalane wale ki zaroorat nahi hai — unhe saaf aaine ki zaroorat hai.

Media ko loktantra ka chautha stambh kaha jata hai — lekin ye stambh aaj hil raha hai. Flarefix usi sabse zaroori khaali jagah ko bharne aaya hai: bina jhukaav, bina shor, bina kisi ke dabav ke — sirf tathya, taaki ye stambh phir se mazboot khada ho sake.

About Flarefix

Flarefix ek daily news podcast hai jo subah ki badi khabron ko shor-sharaabe se alag karke, tathyon ke saath samjhata hai. Har episode mein hum khabar ke peeche ki kahani, uska background, aur aap par uska asar — teeno ko saaf taur par rakhte hain. Na sansani, na pakshpaat. Bas khabar, jaise use hona chahiye.
