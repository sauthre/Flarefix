Why Flarefix?

Television news in India today has become a spectacle. In the race for views and ratings, studios have turned into arenas of shouting — noise, drama, and open bias. Very little news is left, and there is far too much noise.

We believe a newsroom's job is not to hand down verdicts. Its job is to place the facts before the public. People are wise enough on their own: they will see the facts, understand them, and make their own decisions. They do not need anyone to hold their hand — they need a clear mirror.

The media is called the fourth pillar of democracy — but that pillar is shaking today. Flarefix has come to fill exactly that most-needed gap: no bias, no noise, and no pressure from anyone — only facts, so that this pillar can stand strong again.

About Flarefix

Flarefix is a daily news podcast that separates the morning's biggest stories from the noise and explains them with facts. Every episode brings you the story behind the headline, its background, and what it means for you — laid out clearly. No sensationalism, no bias. Just the news, the way it should be.
