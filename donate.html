Free the Media.
Free Your Will.

News stays free only when no one can buy it.

It is not hard to understand the compulsion of the big media houses. Their costs — studios, salaries, broadcasts — are paid for by advertising and sponsors. And there is an old saying: whoever pays for the news slowly bends it to their will. That is why there is so much noise in the news today, and so little truth.

Flarefix chose the other path. We do not run on any sponsor's, owner's, or pressure group's money. We run only on the support of our readers and listeners — so that no one can ever tell us which story will run and which one will be buried.

Your contribution keeps exactly this freedom alive: the daily research, the work behind the newspaper and the episodes, and the technology that brings the news to you — without owing anyone, and without taking anyone's cue.

Our Golden Principle

We will never compromise our principles. If the support ever ends, we will choose to shut down rather than sell out — this news channel and this newspaper will close, but they will never bend.

It is simple: the day we fall into the money trap, you lose us too. That is why we choose to live on your trust — and we will never let that trust break.

How to Support

The contribution channel (UPI) will be announced here very soon. Until then — listen to Flarefix, share it, and take it to your people. Your time and your voice are contributions too.

Thank you — your trust is our first capital.
