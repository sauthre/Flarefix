FLAREFIX KO SAHYOG KAREIN

Free the Media.
Free Your Will.

Khabar tabhi azaad hoti hai, jab use kharidne wala koi na ho.

Bade media gharon ki majboori samajhna mushkil nahi hai. Unka kharcha — studio, salary, telecast — ads aur sponsors ke paise se chalta hai. Aur ek purani kahawat hai: jo paise deta hai, khabar dheere-dheere usi ke hisaab se jhukne lagti hai. Isiliye aaj news mein shor bahut hai, aur sach kam.

Flarefix ne doosra rasta chuna hai. Hum kisi sponsor, kisi maalik aur kisi dabav ke paise par nahi chalte. Hum chalte hain sirf apne pathakon aur shrotaon ke samarthan par — taaki koi humse ye na keh sake ki kaunsi khabar chalegi aur kaunsi dab jayegi.

Aapka yogdaan seedha isi azaadi ko zinda rakhta hai: roz ki research, akhbaar aur episodes banane ki mehnat, aur woh takneek jisse khabar aap tak pahunchti hai — bina kisi ke ehsaan ke, bina kisi ke ishaare ke.

Hamara Golden Principle

Hum apne siddhanton se kabhi samjhota nahi karenge. Agar kabhi samarthan khatm ho gaya, to hum bikne ke bajaye band ho jana chunenge — ye news channel aur ye akhbaar band ho jayega, lekin jhukega nahi.

Seedhi baat hai: paison ke chakkar mein pad gaye, to aap humein bhi kho doge. Isiliye hum aapke bharose par jeena chunte hain — aur usi bharose ko kabhi tootne nahi denge.

Kaise Sahyog Karein

Yogdaan ka channel (UPI) jald yahin ghoshit hoga. Us pal ka intezaar kijiye — aur tab tak Flarefix ko sunein, aage badhayein, aur apne logon tak pahunchayein. Aapka samay aur aapki awaaz bhi ek yogdaan hai.

Dhanyavaad — aapka bharosa hi hamari pehli punji hai.
